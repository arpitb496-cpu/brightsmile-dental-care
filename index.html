07 FAQ

Clear answers to frequent questions.

Everything you need to know about our treatments, visits, and hygiene standards.

Yes, absolutely. We use computer-controlled targeted local anesthesia and modern rotary instruments with laser disinfection. You feel slight vibration, and over 95% of our patients report zero pain throughout the entire procedure.

We attend to urgent dental emergencies immediately on a walk-in basis. For all scheduled treatments, we strongly encourage pre-booking an appointment to guarantee zero waiting time and dedicated unhurried attention from Dr. Sharma.

Yes. We provide 0% interest flexible EMI options through major credit cards, debit cards, and medical finance partners (Bajaj Finserv, PineLabs) for treatments including Aligners, Dental Implants, and Full Smile Makeovers.

We follow strict international CDC and OSHA guidelines. All reusable instruments undergo a 6-tier ultrasonic wash followed by vacuum Class-B Autoclave sterilization in sealed pouches opened directly in front of the patient. Disposable consumables are always used.
